# AI opportunity assessment

Scales 1 (low)–5 (high). Value, repetition, data quality, automation potential, AI suitability and effort are independent; higher risk/effort is less attractive. Ratings are workshop hypotheses, not empirical scores.

| Process | Value | Repetition | Data quality | Risk | Automation | AI | Effort | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Asset location reconciliation | 5 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 1 | 2 | Rules + physical check; no AI |
| QR checkout validation | 5 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 1 | 2 | Deterministic command |
| Return condition description | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 | Optional AI text structuring; inspection human |
| Damage claim drafting | 4 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 3 | Pilot reviewed AI |
| Photo damage summary | 3 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Defer until privacy/vision evaluation |
| Similar claim search | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | Permission-scoped retrieval pilot |
| Legal liability determination | 5 | 2 | 2 | 5 | 1 | 1 | 5 | Human/legal only |
| Manager financial approval | 5 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 1 | 2 | Rule routing; named human decision |
| Invoice reconciliation | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 2 | 3 | Deterministic matches; human exceptions |
| SLA reminders | 4 | 5 | 5 | 1 | 5 | 1 | 1 | Scheduled rules; no AI |
| Purchase duplication detection | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 2 | 3 | Search/rules first |
| Training FAQ search | 3 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 2 | Curated retrieval; source citations |

Pilot claim drafting only if privacy gates pass and manual drafting baseline shows enough time to save. Use normal validation for IDs, approvals and accounting. Reject autonomous liability even if a vendor model claims confidence. Similarity is relevance, not proof of the cause of damage.
