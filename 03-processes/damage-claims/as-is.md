# Damage claims AS-IS

```mermaid
flowchart LR
 D[Damage noticed] --> C[Call / mail / messaging photo]
 C --> X[Coordinator copies into Excel] --> Q[Ask who owns it]
 Q --> A[Informal approval] --> R[Repair supplier]
 R --> I[Invoice emailed] --> F[Finance searches references]
 F --> Z[Close informally]
```

No consistent owner, state, deadline or evidence boundary. Photos may expose people unintentionally. Estimates and invoice actuals are confused; a manager's email lacks versioned approval context. Closure lacks a documented repair outcome.
