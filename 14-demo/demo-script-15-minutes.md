# Fifteen-minute deep dive

0–2min: context, roles and conflicting needs. 2–4min: reconstruct AS-IS and show Five Whys/MoSCoW. 4–6min: follow FR-04 through traceability and transfer receipt BPMN.

6–9min: architecture, system of record and OpenAPI checkout with idempotency/version conflict. Explain why an approved estimate is not a posted invoice. Show ERP failure/retry and stable reference.

9–11min: claim evidence/review; Manager approval at €6,200 remains pending Finance. Show AI draft review and manual fallback boundary.

11–13min: demonstrate damaged return/history; discuss offline draft conflict, unsafe reissue, migration duplicate and UAT. Walk through rollout/rollback.

13–15min: experienced operator response, adoption evidence, measured baseline and cash/capacity sensitivity. Invite questions about trade-offs and unresolved ERP/tenant constraints. Deep technical questions can use the runbook, NFRs and quality audit.
