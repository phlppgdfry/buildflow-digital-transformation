# Interview questions and credible answers

| Question | Answer direction / evidence |
|---|---|
| Why not build first? | Workshop identifies custody root cause; a UI alone cannot define handoff |
| Why Power Platform? | Microsoft estate/support reuse; licence/UX trade-offs documented, not predetermined |
| What does ERP still do? | Masterdata, commitments, PO and invoice accounting; operational app does not replace finance |
| What if ERP fails after accepting? | Query stable externalRef before resend; reconcile uncertain outcome |
| What if a worker resists? | Respect expertise, observe task, co-design minimum flow and prove benefit in pilot |
| What if no network? | Unconfirmed draft and controlled paper receipt; version check on reconnect |
| Why not let AI assess liability? | Poor evidence and human/financial consequences; AI is optional structure only |
| How do you show value? | Baseline/target/denominator/owner; cash separate from capacity; sensitivity |
| What did you actually implement? | Local Node/SQLite prototype and automated checks; tenant/ERP/Power Automate are designed, not deployed |
| Who signs off? | Business Owner, Warehouse, Finance and IT per gate; analyst coordinates evidence |

Never turn hypothetical stakeholder notes into real employment experience. Explain what you would validate next with a real organisation.
