# What this case demonstrates

Independent portfolio case. These evidence links demonstrate analysis/design and prototype reasoning. Real client facilitation, tenant implementation and production delivery require separate practical evidence.

## I can discover

I design interviews and a workshop around real-style handoffs, conflicting needs and root-cause hypotheses. [Workshop method](../01-discovery/workshop-plan.md), [worked fictional notes](../01-discovery/workshop-notes-example.md).

## I can analyse

I turn custody gaps into stable requirements, acceptance examples and measurable outcomes. [Traceability](../02-requirements/requirements-traceability-matrix.md), [AS-IS](../03-processes/inventory/as-is.md), [TO-BE](../03-processes/inventory/to-be.md).

## I can translate

I connect a business handoff to commands, records and failure handling while preserving decision ownership. [Architecture](../04-solution-design/architecture.md), [ADRs](../04-solution-design/decision-log.md).

## I understand technology

The design addresses APIs, ERP ownership, identity, versions, duplicate delivery and reconciliation. [OpenAPI](../05-integrations/api-contracts/openapi.json), [ERP](../05-integrations/erp-integration.md), [security](../04-solution-design/security.md), [runbook](../05-integrations/operations-runbook.md).

## I can automate

I specify durable approval routing, exception handling and deduplicated reminders. [Power Automate blueprints](../06-automation/power-automate/README.md). These are designs, not deployed tenant flows.

## I can evaluate AI

I assess 12 opportunities and reserve sensitive decisions for humans. [Opportunity matrix](../07-ai/ai-opportunity-assessment.md), [human review](../07-ai/human-in-the-loop.md), [evaluation plan](../07-ai/evaluation.md). The demo uses a mock, not a live Claude service.

## I can deliver change

I plan acceptance, migration, a representative pilot, training, rollback and support. [UAT](../09-testing/uat-scenarios.md), [implementation](../10-implementation/implementation-plan.md), [adoption](../11-change-management/adoption-plan.md). Business execution and sign-off remain unperformed in this fictional case.

## I can measure

I define baselines, denominators and owners, and distinguish capacity from cash. [KPI definitions](../13-kpis/target-kpis.md), [business case](../13-kpis/business-case.md). Targets are illustrative; no realised savings are claimed.
