# Recruiter view - 90 seconds

**Independent Digital Transformation Portfolio Case**

## Business challenge

A fictional Belgian construction group uses spreadsheets and disconnected messages to track equipment and damage. Staff struggle to locate assets, follow claims and reconcile costs.

## My role

I developed an independent case covering stakeholder analysis, process and requirements design, solution boundaries and implementation planning. The workshop records and business data are fictional.

## What I delivered

A connected inventory/claims design with workshop methodology, BPMN, traceability, ERP/API ownership, automation blueprints, reviewed AI and UAT/rollout/change plans. A public prototype demonstrates the core process.

## Technologies and concepts

BPMN, REST/OpenAPI, ERP integration, Power Apps/Dataverse/Entra target design, Power Automate flow blueprints and human review of AI. The working demo uses browser simulation or a local Node/SQLite API.

## Business impact

Illustrative targets: location accuracy 92% to at least 98%; claim resolution 18 to at most 10 business days; same-shift capture 55% to at least 95%. These are measurement proposals, not achieved client results.
