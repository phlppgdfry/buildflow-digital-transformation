# Workshop agenda — 150 minutes

| Minute | Activity | Technique | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–15 | Purpose, scope, rules | Stakeholder mapping | Named decision owners |
| 15–40 | Walk a material loan and damaged return | Process walkthrough | AS-IS with handoffs |
| 40–60 | Identify failure points | Pain-point clustering + 5 Whys | Root-cause hypotheses |
| 60–70 | Break | — | — |
| 70–95 | Map events: dispatched, received, damaged, approved | Lightweight event storming | Event/state boundary |
| 95–120 | Draw TO-BE and exception paths | Process mapping | Custody/claim gates |
| 120–140 | Prioritise and test a story | MoSCoW + acceptance example | Must/Should/Could backlog |
| 140–150 | Decisions and unresolved questions | Round-robin playback | Owners and due dates |

Do not treat majority voting as Finance approval. Unresolved legal/financial questions go to accountable owners. Time-box tool debates; the analyst records them as architecture options.
