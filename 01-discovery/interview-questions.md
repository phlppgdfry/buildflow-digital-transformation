# Interview questions

| Interviewee | Question | Why it matters / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | Show the last tool you could not find. Who last handled it? | Find real handoffs, not opinions |
| Site supervisor | What do you do when no connection is available? | Offline and delayed truth |
| Employee | Which steps require removing gloves or switching app? | Interaction friction |
| Warehouse manager | Who can reserve, dispatch and confirm receipt? | Custody separation |
| Finance | When is an estimate committed, and when is an invoice posted? | Avoid duplicate accounting |
| Procurement | What proof precedes a replacement order? | Link search to duplicate purchases |
| IT | Does ERP accept unique external references and incremental extracts? | Retry/reconciliation feasibility |
| Operations | Who decides unsafe equipment can return to service? | Safety gate |
| Privacy adviser | Which photo subjects and retention purposes are justified? | Minimise personal information |
| Management | Which outcome would stop this pilot? | Measurable go/no-go |

Observe tasks, verify records and replay understanding. Ask how exceptions work and who makes the decision. Do not ask “Do you want AI?” before understanding the task.
