# Problem statements and boundaries

| ID | Problem and affected user | Consequence | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|
| PB-01 | Warehouse cannot establish latest custody | Search time and loss | 30 movements, location audit |
| PB-02 | Return condition is inconsistently captured | Unsafe reissue and hidden repair cost | 20 returns and inspections |
| PB-03 | Claim owner and timestamps missing | Late triage and status chasing | 30 synthetic/real anonymised cases |
| PB-04 | Finance cannot match claim to invoice | Duplicate entries and reconciliation work | Invoice/claim sample |
| PB-05 | Site users lose submissions offline | Low trust and inconsistent adoption | Connectivity/task observations |
| PB-06 | Reporting lacks stable definitions | Decisions from incompatible spreadsheets | Compare three reports |

Numbers above are discovery sample plans. Illustrative baseline appears in [baseline](../13-kpis/baseline.md), not measured evidence. Digital custody supports investigation; it does not establish fault.
