# Influence, impact and engagement

| Role | Influence / impact | Need or concern | Engagement / owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operations manager | High / high | Reliable custody without slow sites | Weekly steering; analyst |
| Warehouse manager | High / high | Workable inspection and fallback | Co-design twice weekly; analyst |
| Warehouse operator | Medium / high | Avoid duplicate entry, usability with gloves | Walkthrough and pilot; key user |
| Site supervisor | High / high | Fast dispatch, poor connectivity | Two field workshops; analyst |
| Mobile employee | Low / high | Clear accountability, no surveillance | Observe 5 tasks; key user |
| Project manager | High / medium | Cost visibility per project | Approval design; Operations |
| Finance | High / high | Estimates vs actual postings, separation | Control review; Finance lead |
| Procurement | Medium / medium | Stop avoidable purchases | Reorder root-cause review; analyst |
| IT manager | High / high | Supportable boundaries, identities | Architecture gate; developer |
| Group management | High / medium | Sustainable benefits | Monthly steering; business owner |
| Supplier | Low / medium | Valid repair references | Pilot repair test; Procurement |
| Privacy adviser | Medium / medium | Photos, access and retention | Pre-pilot review; IT manager |

Low influence is not low importance. Validate this map after interviews; it is a fictional engagement hypothesis.
