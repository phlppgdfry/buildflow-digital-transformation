# Business context

BuildFlow Group is fictional: 3 business units, 320 total employees, 150 operational users, 20 active sites, one central warehouse and 2,000 tracked assets worth €2.5M. It uses a legacy ERP and Microsoft 365. The prototype contains only eight representative assets, not 2,000 production records.

Scope: individually identifiable reusable tools, machines and rental items; stock consumables are excluded. Claims include own assets and third-party damage references. Fleet telemetry, insurance settlement, payroll and full preventive maintenance scheduling are outside the pilot.

Two sponsors share dependencies: Operations owns custody and claims; Finance owns accounting controls. Business owner success is reliable custody and fewer undocumented incidents, not the number of apps installed. Pilot languages: Dutch UI and Dutch training in production; this English portfolio documentation/app supports international reviewers. French training is required before rollout to French-speaking units.

Assumptions are illustrative and must be replaced by measured baseline. Asset ownership does not prove damage liability.
