# Illustrative baseline

**Illustrative assumptions — not real company data.** 2,000 reusable assets, €2.5M financial asset value, 320 total employees of whom 150 operational users, 20 sites, 350 submitted claims/year.

Hypotheses: 80 missing assets/year (4%), average irrecoverable replacement value €600; 4,000 search tasks/year ×18min; claim administration 90min/claim; 18 business days median resolution; €12,000 avoidable duplicate purchases/year. Missing percentage snapshot is a KPI, while annual replacement-cost estimate requires separate incident cohort to avoid double counting.

Actual baseline plan: four-week movement sampling at representative sites, location audit of 100+ stratified assets, ≥30 timed search tasks and anonymised claims/invoice cohort from prior year. Finance validates replacement and duplicate-purchase categories are disjoint; owner signs baseline. Seasonal project mix limits extrapolation.
