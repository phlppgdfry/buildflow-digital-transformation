# Benefits realisation plan

Baseline owner Warehouse/Finance signs four-week sample. Pilot tests adoption/data integrity; benefit trends at 30/60/90 days compare similar site mix, volume and season. Business Owner owns overall benefits, Finance validates financial amounts, analyst maintains definitions/evidence.

For loss benefits use verified irrecoverable replacement incidents, not all temporary Lost statuses. Keep duplicate purchase category disjoint. Capacity benefits require observation of search/admin task time and a agreed use for released hours. If task time falls but hours cannot be redeployed, report time benefit without cash.

At 90 days: accept outcome, adjust target or reduce scope. Document confounders (project closures, asset disposals, claim mix) and missed targets. No benefit is marked realised in this fictional case.
