# Hypercare — first ten working days

Warehouse champion onsite each shift; coordinator handles claim questions; IT support checks queue/errors at 08:00/12:00/16:00. Finance reconciles commitments/invoices daily. Analyst gathers friction and links change requests to requirements.

Daily 15-minute triage: physical custody mismatches, unsafe assets, approval mapping, outage entries, evidence access and adoption. Sev1 immediately stops affected command/dispatch; Sev2 repaired or mitigated before wave expansion; cosmetic requests join backlog.

Exit after five consecutive working days with no Sev1/2, queue freshness within target, no unexplained cost discrepancy, ≥95% movement capture and signed support handover. Record runbook, known limitations, service owner and residual backlog. If adoption drops, observe user tasks before asking for more training.
