# Escalation ownership

At deadline breach, notify coordinator and Operations. If approval mapping is absent, Operations assigns an authorised delegate. High-value finance decision remains Finance-owned. If ERP pending >15min IT triages; >1h Finance checks repair impact. Escalation never approves on behalf of an absent person and never automatically labels someone liable.

SLA uses business calendar, not naive elapsed days. Original resolution deadline remains for Information Required and blocked supplier cases; record blocker separately. [AUT-03](power-automate/03-overdue.md).
