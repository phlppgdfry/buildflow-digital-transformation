# Notification design

Messages contain claim/asset ID, owner, deadline and authenticated link. No photos, liability accusation, personal phone details or repair invoice attachment in Teams/email. Send reminders only once per claim/stage/window; delivery failures are operational events. Users can choose channel preferences for reminders, but mandatory safety alerts still follow Operations policy. Critical damage requires immediate phone escalation in addition to asynchronous message.
