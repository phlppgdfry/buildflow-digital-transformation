# Approval rules

Manager owns operational repair approval. Finance adds a named decision above €5,000; Operations is notified. Reporter cannot approve own claim. Both decisions bind to claim revision and amount; estimate/evidence changes require reapproval. Delegation has scope/expiry and audit. Pending Finance does not become Approved just because the Manager accepted.

Durable tasks and separate response processing avoid waiting across long claim lifecycles. Failed notifications do not remove the task. [AUT-02](power-automate/02-high-value.md).
