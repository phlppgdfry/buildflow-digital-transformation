# Communication plan

| When | Audience / channel | Message | Owner / feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Warehouse/site walkthrough | We want to understand workarounds before choosing tools | Analyst; interviews |
| Two weeks pre-pilot | Shift briefing + one-page guide | Scope, privacy, custody and fallback | Warehouse manager; open Q&A |
| Pilot launch | Site champion + demo | Three core tasks, support and no blame | Key User; observe tasks |
| Pilot daily | 15min huddle | What worked, blockers and action owners | Analyst; issue board |
| Evaluation | Steering + user playback | Measured results, limitations and Go/extend/stop | Business Owner; recorded decision |
| Rollout wave | Local language sessions | Changes specific to this site and contact | Operations; feedback form |
| 30/60/90 days | Management + user summary | Outcomes vs baseline, time saved vs cash | Business Owner; benefits review |

All illustrative dates use relative milestones. Do not announce savings before measurement or promise monitoring of individual employee performance.
