# Adoption and respectful resistance response

**Fictional objection:** “Het oude Excelbestand werkt al tien jaar, waarom moeten we dit veranderen?”

Analyst response: “Dat bestand bevat duidelijk veel praktische kennis. Kun je tonen hoe je een rechtstreekse transfer tussen twee werven opvolgt? Ik wil begrijpen wat goed werkt en waar jij tijd verliest.” Listen and walk through the current process before proposing a replacement.

Show one concrete failure without blaming: an unrecorded loan leads to searching and buying again. Ask the experienced operator to design the minimum scan/receipt steps and outage form. Compare old/new task time in a small pilot. If the new flow adds work without improving custody, simplify it. Keep a visible feedback log, name action owners and play back improvements.

Champions coach peers; training is task-based and scheduled during work. During outages the fallback is controlled and later reconciled. Avoid parallel authoritative sheets indefinitely; remove duplicate entry only when pilot controls and support work.

Adoption measures: ≥95% same-shift capture (KPI-14), ≥90% active trained pilot users/week and ≥95% observed three-task success (KPI-15). These are targets. Segment by role/site for improvement, never employee blame rankings. Weekly observation and anonymous feedback are as important as login counts.
