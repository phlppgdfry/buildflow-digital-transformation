# Business decision log

| ID | Context/options | Decision / rationale | Owner / consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEC-01 | Dispatch vs receipt custody | Custody transfers on confirmed receipt | Operations; sender retains In Transit accountability |
| DEC-02 | Single vs dual high-value approval | Manager plus Finance strictly above €5,000 | Finance; more control, possible delay |
| DEC-03 | Offline authoritative vs draft | Draft + controlled paper fallback | Warehouse/Operations; reconcile on reconnection |
| DEC-04 | AI decisions vs assistance | Optional human-reviewed drafts | Business Owner; evaluate value before deployment |
| DEC-05 | All sites vs pilot | One warehouse + two sites first | Business Owner; gradual benefits, lower disruption |

All are fictional agreed decisions for this case, not signed client approvals. Technical alternatives and consequences are in [ADRs](../04-solution-design/decision-log.md). Changes require update to requirements, tests, risks and user guidance.
