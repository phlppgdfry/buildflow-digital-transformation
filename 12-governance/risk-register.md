# Project risk register

| Risk | L×I | Prevent / contingency | Owner | Residual |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Poor asset master | 4×4 | Quarantine + physical sweep; delay pilot population | Warehouse | Cleansing effort |
| ERP duplicate/unknown response | 3×5 | Stable reference + lookup/reconcile; quarantine | IT / Finance | Vendor capability |
| User adoption | 3×4 | Co-design/task practice; extend pilot | Operations | Shift capacity |
| Unsafe equipment released | 2×5 | Inspection/state gate; stop checkout if control fails | Warehouse | Human inspection quality |
| Personal evidence exposure | 3×5 | Scope/redaction/scan; restrict upload | IT/privacy | Client policy review |
| Connectivity | 4×3 | Draft + paper ledger; reconcile | IT | Delayed visibility |
| Licence/support costs | 3×3 | Priced options + support rota; reconsider target | IT Manager | Tenant costs |
| Unproven benefits | 3×4 | Baseline and 90-day study; phase investment | Business Owner | Sample uncertainty |

Risk scores are prioritisation hypotheses. Assess residual risk after evidence, and document acceptance by the accountable owner. AI-specific risks are in [AI register](../07-ai/ai-risk-register.md).
