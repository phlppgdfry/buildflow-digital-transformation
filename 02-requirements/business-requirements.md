# Business requirements

| ID | Problem | Required outcome | Accountable owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | PB-01 | Every active asset has demonstrable custody/location; reduce missing assets | Operations |
| BR-02 | PB-02 | Unsafe equipment cannot be reissued without inspection/release | Warehouse manager |
| BR-03 | PB-03 | Claims have ownership, evidence and timely triage | Operations |
| BR-04 | PB-04 | Repair commitments and actual invoices remain controlled and reconcilable | Finance |
| BR-05 | PB-05 | Field staff can work during outages without false confirmations | Operations |
| BR-06 | PB-06 | Managers use consistent metrics and data-quality context | Business owner |

Must outcomes precede AI or reporting enhancements. A prioritisation change requires owner agreement and a traceability update; cost control cannot be traded for a faster demo.
